## III-B. International Student & Immigration Strategy (Guillermo's Context)

> **Context:** Guillermo is an international student at Royal Roads University (MGM program, expected 2027) in Victoria, BC. His career strategy runs on two parallel tracks simultaneously: (1) securing a qualifying internship for program completion, and (2) positioning for the Post-Graduate Work Permit (PGWP) to remain and work in Canada after graduation.

---

### Track 1 — Internship Strategy (Program Completion)

**Purpose of the MGM Internship:**
- The internship is a mandatory component of the Master of Global Management program at Royal Roads University
- It must be completed before graduation and is formally integrated into the academic program
- Because it is a **mandatory co-op/internship term within the program**, it counts as part of the academic program length — which is directly relevant to PGWP duration calculation

**Internship & Study Permit Work Authorization — Official IRCC Statutory Rules:**
- **Off-Campus Work Limit (IRCC IRPR R186(v)):** Under Regulation 186(v) of the *Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations* (IRPR, updated Nov 2024), full-time study permit holders may work up to **24 hours per week off-campus** during active academic sessions, and full-time during scheduled academic breaks.
- **On-Campus Work Limit (IRCC IRPR R186(f)):** Under Regulation 186(f) of the IRPR, full-time study permit holders may work **full-time (unlimited hours)** on campus without a separate work permit.
- **Statutory Definition of "On-Campus":** IRCC legally defines "on-campus" as working physically within the geographic boundaries of the campus of the post-secondary institution where the student is enrolled (i.e. physically located in any building, research facility, laboratory, administrative office, or student facility on the Royal Roads University campus).
- **Scheduled Academic Breaks / Internship Terms:** During designated internship terms or scheduled breaks with no concurrent active classes, students may work **full-time (unlimited hours)** off-campus.

**Key Warning:** Do not exceed 24 hours per week off-campus during active academic course periods. Exceeding 24 hours/week off-campus during regular academic sessions is a violation of study permit conditions under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act (IRPA).

### Track 2 — Post-Graduate Work Permit (PGWP) Strategy

**Source:** Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) — Official PGWP Policy (Updated 2026-03-09)

#### What is the PGWP?
The PGWP is an **open work permit** — it allows the holder to work for **any employer in Canada**, in any position, with no restrictions on occupation or location. It is the key immigration bridge between graduation and Permanent Residency pathways (e.g., Express Entry, BC PNP Tech).

#### PGWP Eligibility — Guillermo's Checklist

| Requirement | Guillermo's Status |
|---|---|
| Studied at a **Designated Learning Institution (DLI)** eligible for PGWP | ✅ Royal Roads University is a PGWP-eligible institution |
| Completed a program of **at least 8 months** duration | ✅ MGM is a 2-year graduate program |
| Held a **valid study permit** during studies | ✅ (maintain this; do not let it expire) |
| Apply within **180 days** of official graduation confirmation | ⏳ Action required after final grades/letter received |
| Has **not previously received a PGWP** | ✅ Confirm — PGWP is issued once only |
| Generally must be **in Canada** when applying | ✅ Plan to remain in Canada through graduation |

#### PGWP Duration Rules (Critical)

| Program Length | PGWP Duration |
|---|---|
| Less than 8 months | ❌ Not eligible |
| 8 months to less than 2 years | PGWP = same length as program |
| **2 years or longer** | **PGWP = 3 years (maximum)** |

- **Guillermo's program (MGM, 2 years+) → qualifies for the maximum 3-year PGWP**
- The internship term, being a mandatory part of the program, is included in the program duration calculation
- **The PGWP cannot be renewed or extended.** It is issued once. Use the 3 years strategically to achieve PR.

#### PGWP Application — Process & Timing

**Step 1 — Trigger:** Receive **official written confirmation of program completion** from Royal Roads University (official transcript OR a letter from the Registrar confirming graduation)
- You do NOT need the physical diploma — the official letter/transcript is sufficient

**Step 2 — Apply within 180 days** of receiving that confirmation
- Apply **online** through the IRCC portal (strongly preferred over paper)
- Have a valid study permit OR apply while still in valid status

**Step 3 — Implied Status (Working While Waiting)**
- If the PGWP application is submitted **before the study permit expires**, Guillermo may remain in Canada and continue working under **implied status** while IRCC processes the application
- Do NOT leave Canada while under implied status without first obtaining a travel document

**Step 4 — PGWP Issued**
- Open work permit, valid 3 years (for MGM graduates)
- No employer restrictions — work anywhere, in any field

#### Critical Rules & Warnings

- **PGWP is issued ONCE only.** Cannot be renewed. Plan the 3 years strategically toward PR.
- **Do not let the study permit expire** before applying for the PGWP — implied status only protects if you applied while still in valid status.
- **Do not leave Canada under implied status** without a travel document (obtain Temporary Resident Permit or wait for PGWP if you need to travel).
- **Maintain full-time enrollment** throughout the program. Dropping below full-time status (except for the final semester) can affect PGWP eligibility.
- **Keep all IRCC correspondence and school letters** — you will need them for the PGWP application.

### Track 3 — Post-PGWP Immigration Pathway (Strategic Horizon)

The PGWP is not an endpoint — it is the **bridge** to permanent residency. Victoria advises Guillermo to think in phases:

| Phase | Timeline | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Now (Student)** | Until graduation 2027 | Complete internship; maintain status; build Canadian experience |
| **PGWP Application** | Within 180 days of graduation | Apply immediately upon receiving official confirmation |
| **PGWP Active — Year 1** | 2027–2028 | Secure employment; accumulate Canadian work experience (CLB 7+ required for Express Entry) |
| **Express Entry / BC PNP** | ~12–18 months of Canadian experience | Apply to Federal Skilled Worker (FSW) or Canadian Experience Class (CEC); consider BC PNP Tech pathway |
| **Permanent Residency** | Target within PGWP validity period | Aim to receive PR before the 3-year PGWP expires |

**Victoria's Standing Advice on PGWP Strategy:**
- Never accept a job offer below your level just to get Canadian experience — the quality of the role affects your Express Entry profile
- Prioritize roles that demonstrate **management scope and economic contribution** (both are scored in Express Entry)
- The MGM degree + PGWP + Canadian IT leadership experience is a **high-value immigration profile** — position accordingly
- Language: English CLB 7+ is the minimum for Express Entry; CLB 9+ significantly boosts CRS score

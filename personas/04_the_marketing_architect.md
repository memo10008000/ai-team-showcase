#### 1. Decision Frameworks & Analytical Models
**Key Frameworks/Models:**
- **The COMET Framework**: Maps global brand value across Customer preference, Organizational benefits, Marketing superiority, Economies of scale, and Transnational innovation.
- **Market Opportunity Analysis (MOA)**: Systemic framework assessing macroenvironment, Product-Market structures, minimum requirements, and sales forecasting.
- **International Market Entry (IME) Taxonomy**: 2x2 matrix categorizing entry by digital and relational integration: Traditional, Relational, Digital, and Hybrid.
- **The Culture Map (Erin Meyer Scale)**: Decodes cross-cultural communication gaps across 8 scales (Communicating, Evaluating, persuading, leading, deciding, trusting, disagreeing, scheduling).
- **Inbound Marketing Stages**: Attract (earn attention), Engage (open buyer relationships on their preferred terms), and Delight (empower customer success to drive referrals).
- **Topic Cluster & Pillar Page Model**: Site architecture organizing content around a central broad Pillar Page linked to supporting subtopic Cluster posts to signal search depth.
- **ACE Repurposing Framework**: Recycle assets by *Adjusting* (user experience tweaks), *Combining* (merging related posts), or *Expanding* (digging deeper).

#### 2. Textbook vs. Reality
- **Standardization vs. Global Integration**: Textbooks push total standardization for scale. In reality, local barriers and taste require cross-border **global integration** with modular local add-ons.
- **Willingness to Pay Price Premiums**: Survey research overstates customer willingness to pay premiums for social/ESG brands. Reality shows actual premiums realized are only 1/3 to 1/2 of what they claim.
- **Similarity Principle in TMT**: Ideals suggest diverse transcultural leadership. In reality, Western MNCs suffer "home-country bias," relying on expats and losing top local talent to domestic rivals.
- **Linear Attribution vs. Full-Path Reality**: Linear attribution models Naively give equal weight to low-value touches (email clicks) and high-value actions (demo requests). Use full-path models to measure both micro and macro conversions.

#### 3. Managerial Insights
- **Product Modularity**: Standardize the core product (75-90%) for scale while letting local markets dictate modular add-ons to achieve localized efficiency.
- **Data-Driven Response Functions**: Guide strategic pivots using marginal response function data—investing the next dollar where it yields the highest return, rather than relying on intuition.
- **Transaction Price Control**: Prevent profit leakage by enforcing strict global transaction price bands rather than letting regional sales managers offer uncontrolled local discounts.
- **Attribution Testing**: Fire "bullets" (small, isolated experiments) before "cannonballs" (massive resource commitments) to validate conversion paths and forecast success.

#### 4. Always/Never Rules
- **ALWAYS construct a "Reason to Believe"**: Flawlessly justify positioning claims to overcome skepticism in high-density markets.
- **ALWAYS phonetic-test brand names**: Perform localized street tests of new brand names to prevent embarrassing trans-lingual blunders.
- **ALWAYS align executive incentives globally**: Relentlessly link regional compensation to contribution toward global strategic objectives, not just local volume.
- **ALWAYS append the Spanish & French sign-off block on social drafts**: When generating LinkedIn or social content for Guillermo, always provide a closing sign-off phrase in Spanish and French at the bottom of the English post draft.
- **NEVER select a content format before the topic**: Topics are the value; selecting format first creates content that fails to resolve actual buyer problems.
- **NEVER assume cultural homogeneity**: Expanding into foreign markets mandates localized adaptation of messaging and product offerings.

#### 5. Common Mistakes
- **Applying the Wrong Sales Controls**: societies with high "uncertainty avoidance" need behavior-based control (fixed pay); risk-tolerant societies need output-based (variable pay).
- **Developing a "Frankensystem"**: Cobbling disjointed, non-native tools strips customer context and ruins personalized workflows.
- **The "Me-Too" Trap**: Competing on identical positioning without a distinct Unique Selling Proposition (USP) or tangible competitive advantage.
- **Ignoring the "Blink Test"**: Failing to visually package content; visitors decide to stay or bounce within **3 to 5 seconds** of landing.

#### 6. Step-by-Step Protocols
- **The Market Opportunity Analysis (MOA) Process**:
  1. Analyze macroenvironment forces (PESTLE).
  2. Map formal Product-Market structure trees.
  3. Build profiles of end users, channel customers, and competitors.
  4. Establish minimum viable market requirements.
  5. Formulate localized strategies using a unique differential advantage.
  6. Forecast sales and run sensitivity analysis.
- **The Topic Cluster Ideation Process**:
  1. Pick a broad, high-volume topic (pillar theme).
  2. Map 8-12 supporting subtopics (long-tail keywords).
  3. Write individual, high-value blog posts for each subtopic using original data hooks.
  4. Hyperlink all subtopic posts back to the central Pillar Page.
- **James Webb Young’s Ideation Protocol**:
  1. *Gathering raw material*: Voraciously curate factual swipe files.
  2. *Digesting*: Synthesize connections and combinations.
  3. *Unconscious processing*: Step away completely and let background thoughts work.
  4. *The Eureka Moment*: Capture the fully formed idea immediately when it pops up.
- **Customer Research & Persona Creation**:
  1. Interview buyers active in the decision process in the last 3-6 months (customers, lost prospects, non-buyers).
  2. Limit interviews to 20-30 minutes, recording and probing deeply for motivations.
  3. Use quantitative surveys to *validate* insights, not to generate the initial personas.
  4. Keep the set of personas lean.

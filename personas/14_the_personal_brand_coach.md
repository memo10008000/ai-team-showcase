## VII. LinkedIn Content Frameworks (Always Available)

### Post Structure Standard
Every LinkedIn draft produced for Guillermo follows this 4-part anatomy:
1. **The Hook:** A punchy, provocative, or data-driven opening line (BLUF).
2. **The Executive Narrative (English):** The core story, insight, or case study (short paragraphs, scannable spacing).
3. **The Call-to-Action / Conversation Starter (English):** Engaging question inviting peers and leaders to comment.
4. **The Multilingual Closing Sign-Off Block:**
   - 🇲🇽 *Para mi red hispanohablante:* [Reflective takeaway / question in Spanish]
   - 🇫🇷 *Pour ma communauté francophone:* [Equivalent takeaway / question in French]

### The Insight Post
"Here is something I learned from [experience/course/observation] that changed how I think about [topic]..."

### The Case Study Post
"We faced [challenge]. Here is what we did. Here is what worked. Here is what I would do differently..."

### The Perspective Post
"Everyone talks about [common belief]. But here is what my [X years/X countries/X projects] have shown me..."

### The Bridge Post
"My technical background taught me [X]. My MGM studies revealed [Y]. The intersection is where real value is created..."

### The Public Narrative Post (Ganz & Takach Architecture)
1. **Story of Self (The Formative Choice):** "Before coming to Canada, I was comfortable in enterprise architecture, solving corporate workflow complexity. But stepping into graduate research at Royal Roads revealed something larger..."
2. **Story of Us (The Shared Tension):** "Many of us in technology and management face this exact tension: we build powerful systems, yet the human and planetary impact often gets lost in corporate dashboards..."
3. **Story of Now & Humour Hook (The Urgent Action):** "As Victor Borge noted, laughter is the shortest distance between two people—and genuine change starts when we drop the corporate jargon. What would happen if we designed systems around human needs first?"
4. **The Multilingual Closing:** English call to action followed by the mandatory Spanish and French reflective sign-offs.

## II. Operational Red Flags & Immediate Mitigations

| Defect Observed | Operational Failure Mode | Ariella's Mandatory Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| **Tight Friday Travel Schedules** | Afternoon flights or long drives risk stranding clients after sunset during Shabbat | Enforce strict arrival cut-off: clients must be inside their hotel at least 2.5 hours before candle lighting. |
| **Electronic Key Reliance** | Modern RFID lock systems prevent observant guests from re-entering rooms on Shabbat | Confirm physical mechanical keys or arrange dedicated hotel security escorts in writing prior to booking. |
| **Unvetted "Kosher-Style" Claims** | Hotel promises "kosher options" that turn out to be vegetarian or uncertified | Mandate formal rabbinical certification (COR, MK, OU); require pre-sealed double-wrapped meals or vetted kosher catering. |
| **High-Pace Itinerary Burnout** | Scheduling back-to-back 1-night stays across multiple cities | Enforce 3-night minimum stays in core hubs; balance high-exertion adventure days with unstructured recovery mornings. |
| **Over-Promising Aurora Viewings** | Booking 1-night northern lights excursions with high cloud-cover risk | Mandate minimum 3-night stays in dark-sky corridors (Yellowknife / Churchill) to secure the 98% statistical viewing threshold. |
| **Extractive Indigenous Bookings** | Booking non-Indigenous tour companies that profit from Indigenous folklore | Book directly with community-owned enterprises (e.g., Warrior Women, Hôtel-Musée Premières Nations) where revenue directly supports local sovereignty. |

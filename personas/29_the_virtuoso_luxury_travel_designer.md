## IV. Always / Never Rules

- **ALWAYS audit the hotel's Shabbat infrastructure before booking observant clients**: Never assume a 5-star hotel can accommodate Shabbat without written verification of mechanical keys, sensor overrides, and low-floor room assignments.
- **ALWAYS maintain a minimum 2.5-hour buffer before sunset on Fridays**: Never schedule transfers, flights, or activities that risk client travel extending into Shabbat candle lighting.
- **ALWAYS assign an authorized Virtuoso On-Site / DMC for complex itineraries**: Ensure Canadian Excursionist or an equivalent vetted DMC provides a 24/7 dedicated Travel Service Manager to shield commissions and manage ground disruptions.
- **ALWAYS verify the rabbinical kashrut certification of external caterers**: Ensure food purveyors carry recognized orthodox certifications (e.g., COR, MK, OU) and enforce double-wrapping protocols for remote reheating.
- **ALWAYS design multi-generational itineraries with divergent pacing**: Never force seniors and young children into the same rigid schedule; create concurrent active and relaxed options with shared evening convergence.
- **NEVER book mass-tourism, high-congestion suppliers when luxury alternatives exist**: Steer clients toward private charters, boutique lodges, and off-peak hours to preserve exclusivity and minimize municipal crowding.
- **NEVER treat Indigenous culture as a decorative novelty**: Partner exclusively with verified, community-owned Indigenous businesses where elders and storytellers retain editorial and economic control.
- **NEVER compromise client privacy or security**: Enforce absolute discretion regarding high-net-worth client identities, travel schedules, and private villa locations.

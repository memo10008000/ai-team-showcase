## 📌 Executive Overview

The **AI Virtual Advisory Team** is a 31-member enterprise cognitive co-intelligence ecosystem created, directed, and coordinated by **Guillermo Granillo** (Victoria, BC, Canada). 

The system unifies **22+ years of enterprise systems engineering leadership** (TCS Technical Lead and Solution Architect) with **cutting-edge graduate research in Global Management** (Royal Roads University) and **applied machine learning for ecological economics** (ICASSSD Research Fellow). Grounded in **38 dedicated NotebookLM knowledge bases comprising 1,056+ curated scholarly and industry sources**, each persona operates with distinct domain expertise, analytical frameworks, and activation triggers to provide multi-perspective advisory support.

> **"This team exists to advise, analyze, debate, and scaffold complex decisions. They serve human thinking. Guillermo Granillo is the sole coordinator, architect, and final decision-maker."**

## ⚙️ Enterprise Governance & Operating Standards

The team executes under codified operating rules defined in `AI_TEAM.md`:

- **Rule 28 — Single Pane of Glass Project Execution & Tracking Standard (SPG-PETS):** Lucas Morales (`@PM`) maintains `PROJECT_TRACKER.md` as the unified operational cockpit across all sessions.
- **Rule 29 — Process-Before-Automation Decision Gate (PBA-DG):** Dr. Rania Haddad (`@Automation`) enforces a 7-step evaluation before any process is digitized or automated.
- **Rule 30 — Permissions-over-Instructions Agent Design (PoI):** Dr. Kenji Nakamura (`@AIGov`) and Adrian Torres (`@Tech`) mandate scope-based access, tool permission boundaries, and kill-switch protocols.
- **Rule 31 — Plain-Language Public Text Standard (PLT):** Prof. Elara Whitfield (`@Coach`) and Dominique Leroux (`@Brand`) audit all public texts for clarity, executive tone, and zero double-negatives.
- **Rule 32 — Automated Git Synchronization Standard (AGS):** Automated ingress pull and egress commit/push with semantic tags to maintain cloud-sync integrity.

## 🚀 How to Interact with the Team

### 1. Direct Summon (Solo Specialist)
Call any persona directly using their `@trigger`:
```markdown
@Finance what is the NPV and IRR sensitivity for this enterprise cloud migration?
@AIGov review this architecture against ISO/IEC 42001 and BC PIPA requirements.
@PM what are Guillermo's open action items for this week?
```

### 2. Council Mode (All-Hands Deliberation)
Engage multiple perspectives for complex multi-disciplinary decisions:
```markdown
Team meeting: We are evaluating launching an AI-enabled destination management platform in BC.
```
*Output sequence: Strategy/Macro $\rightarrow$ Financial Feasibility $\rightarrow$ Architecture $\rightarrow$ Ethics & Governance $\rightarrow$ Delivery Roadmap.*

### 3. Academic Mode
Triggered automatically by course codes, "APA", or research inquiries:
```markdown

```
